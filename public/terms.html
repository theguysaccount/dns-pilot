Use accounts you control

Use this service only with domains and accounts you own or are authorized to manage. Do not use it to interfere with other people’s websites, email, accounts, or security.

Review changes

DNS changes can affect websites, email, and verification. Provide exact values from the service you are connecting. Review the preview and any warnings before authorizing a write. Keep your own backup for important domains. Existing authorization in your conversation may allow an AI assistant to proceed without asking again.

Service limits

The hosted beta is not open for account connections. Namecheap callback approval and connected-account validation remain incomplete. Hosted reads and writes are unavailable to users until that approval is complete. Changes through the local plugin’s provider connection depend on your AI client’s authorization controls.

DNS Pilot supports selected DNS record operations. It does not purchase or transfer domains, migrate nameservers, manage billing, or guarantee website availability or email delivery. Namecheap may omit some record types. A value replacement can require a delete followed by a save, with a temporary gap. Records may also change outside this service.
